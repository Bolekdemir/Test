# Mert app surface

Scope: the whole app (home, setup, pass/reveal, discussion, vote, result, game over). Mode: Operate (players complete a game), with Experience moments at reveal and result.

Audience/job: friend groups passing one iPad; set up fast, peek privately, talk, vote.
Constraints: user-pinned Apple-like feel and sage/eucalyptus palette + cream + white. Roles must never leak.
Memorable moment: lifting the secret card to peek, and the drenched-color verdict after a vote.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mert is a calm still life on a side table: matte ceramic, linen, smoked green glass. Refuses the party-game default of neon, confetti and cartoon spies.

OWN-WORLD: Linen cream ground, chalk-white ceramic surfaces with soft offset shadows, pale mint and sage fills for selection, deep forest ink. Smoked green glass (deep forest, translucent) is reserved for anything secret. A muted terracotta is the imposter's color, used only for imposter truth. System SF Pro, tight display tracking, tabular numerals.

STORY: Open, tap New game, add names, tap topics, set imposters and timer, start. Pass the iPad, lift the glass card to peek, let go, pass. Talk under one countdown, vote any time, watch the verdict fill the screen, play again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the wordmark "Mert" huge, left-aligned in the upper third on linen; beneath, one chalk ceramic primary button "New game" and a quiet "How to play". A stack of three face-down glass cards sits fanned at the lower right as the only object.

FORM: Tabletop still life (candidate 7 of 7, assigned). Seed key 1ae3338d. User-pinned Apple-native grammar governs controls and navigation. Raise from split-flap concourse (declined): numbers change in fixed cells with a single coordinated roll, never reflowing. Raise from zoo guide map (declined): verdict screens commit color at full-screen scale instead of accents.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
