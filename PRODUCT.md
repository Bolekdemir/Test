# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React + Vite + TypeScript, Motion for spring physics, installable as a home-screen web app (PWA). Chosen because it runs in iPad Safari with no build step for the players and supports interruptible, physics-based motion.

## Users

Friend groups (mostly teens and young adults) sitting together in one room, passing a single iPad around. One person usually sets up the game; everyone handles the device for a few seconds each to see their secret role.

## Product Purpose

Mert runs the party word game "Imposter" on one shared device. Everyone except the imposter(s) learns a secret word; players take turns saying one related word out loud; the group votes out who they think the imposter is. The app handles what paper can't: secret role assignment, the pass-around reveal, a discussion timer, and tracking votes until a side wins. Success: a group goes from opening the app to playing in under a minute, nobody accidentally sees someone else's role, and people want "one more round".

## Positioning

A single-device, pass-and-play Imposter game that feels like a native Apple app: calm, tactile, premium, no ads, no accounts, works offline.

## Operating Context

- Played in person; all talking happens out loud. The app never needs to hear or type words during play.
- One iPad passed hand to hand, often in a living room, often in the evening.
- Each player looks at the screen privately for a few seconds; others are physically close, so the reveal must be hard to peek at.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Setup: freely add, rename, and remove players; pick one or more topics (large catalog, including "All", games, brainrot and many more); choose imposter count; choose a discussion time limit (or none).
- Imposters get no information at all: no word, no topic.
- Imposters do not know each other.
- No last-chance word guess for a caught imposter.
- Discussion has one overall countdown; players can start the vote at any time.
- Crew wins when all imposters are voted out; imposters win when they are at least as many as the remaining crew.
- English only. No online multiplayer in v1. No accounts, no server; everything stored on device.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Mert**.
- Feel: simple, very easy to understand, premium and Apple-like, clean, smooth, high-quality animation.
- Palette (user-supplied, binding): soft light grey, pale mint, light sage, sage, deep forest green, plus cream and pure white; a few supporting colors may be added.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied palette reference image (sage/eucalyptus interior). No logo, no other assets yet.

## Product Principles

1. The secret is sacred: no flow, gesture, or bug may reveal one player's role to another.
2. Zero learning curve: every screen has one obvious next action.
3. The device is a game piece: it should feel physical to hold, pass, and touch.
4. Get out of the way of the conversation: the app paces the game, the people play it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large tap targets for a device being passed around; respect reduced motion; legible at arm's length.
